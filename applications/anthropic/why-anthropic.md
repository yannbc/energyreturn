# Why Anthropic?

*(For the application form field -- they value this highly, 200-400 words)*

I want to work at Anthropic because you're the company that treats safety as an engineering constraint rather than a marketing claim, and I think that distinction matters enormously for enterprise adoption in ANZ.

I've spent the last four years shipping AI systems that talk directly to real customers -- at Pendula, our agentic workflows handled production conversations where a wrong output wasn't an embarrassment, it was a compliance event. That experience taught me that the companies who win enterprise AI deals aren't the ones with the best benchmarks. They're the ones who can answer the question: "What happens when it goes wrong?"

Anthropic answers that question more credibly than anyone. Constitutional AI, interpretability research, the responsible scaling policy -- these aren't academic exercises. They're the exact things I needed when I was sitting across the table from an enterprise CTO in a regulated industry, trying to get them comfortable putting an AI system in front of their customers. I didn't have those tools then. Building the team that brings them to ANZ enterprises is a problem I'm genuinely motivated to solve.

The ANZ market is also at an inflection point. The big banks, telcos, and government agencies are past the "should we use AI?" conversation and into the "how do we deploy it without getting burned?" conversation. That second conversation favours Anthropic's positioning, but only if there's a local team that can do the technical work of scoping, building POCs, and showing enterprise buyers what responsible deployment actually looks like. I've done that work in energy, communications, and fintech -- and I know what it takes to build and lead the team that does it at scale.

I'm also drawn to the intellectual honesty of the culture. I co-founded a company whose ML platform enabled three peer-reviewed papers in top economics journals. I care about rigour, and I want to work somewhere that does too.
